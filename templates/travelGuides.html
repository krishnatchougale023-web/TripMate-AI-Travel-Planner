Travel Safe: Guidelines & Rules

Essential rules and smart tips to make your journey smooth and secure.

1. Before You Leave

  • Documents: Always carry soft copies of your ID proofs, visas, and tickets on your phone/cloud storage.
  • Medical Kit: Keep a basic medical kit with pain relievers, band-aids, and your regular prescriptions.
  • Emergency Contacts: Save local emergency numbers of your destination beforehand.

2. Respect Local Culture

  • Dress Codes: Follow appropriate dress codes when visiting religious or sacred places.
  • Eco-Friendly: Do not litter. Keep the environment clean and respect wildlife.
  • Photography: Seek permission before taking photos of locals or restricted government properties.

3. Safety Warnings

  • Public Wi-Fi: Avoid making banking transactions while connected to public or railway station Wi-Fi network.
  • Scams: Beware of unauthorized tour guides or extremely cheap, suspicious travel deals.
  • Valuables: Never leave your luggage or expensive gadgets unattended in public transport.
